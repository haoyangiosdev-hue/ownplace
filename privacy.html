Ownplace Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-26

Ownplace ("the app") is developed by yang hao ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information.

1. We don't run servers and don't collect your data.

The app connects only to a server that you own or rent ("your node"). We do not operate any server that receives your conversations, files, memories or usage. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and we do not create accounts.

2. Where your data lives.

Conversations, records saved by roles, photos you share, and memories are stored on your node. You hold administrator access to it; we do not. On your phone, the app stores the node's address, its certificate fingerprint and access keys in the system keychain, and your display preferences locally.

3. Setting up your node.

When you connect a server, the app logs in over SSH using the password you enter, installs the node software, and replaces the password with a key generated on your phone. The password is used only for that login and is not stored or sent anywhere else. The server downloads the node software from our download host; that host serves public program files and receives no personal data, although, like any web server, its provider may log the requesting server's IP address.

4. AI models and consent.

You choose the AI model each role uses. If it is your own inference server, data stays within infrastructure you control. If it is a third-party provider, the app asks for your consent for that specific role and provider before anything is sent; without consent nothing is sent. Once you consent, the relevant context is sent from your node directly to that provider under your own API key, and is governed by that provider's terms and privacy policy. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app.

5. Friends.

If you add a friend, messages and files travel directly between your node and theirs, end-to-end encrypted. They are not stored by us.

6. Reporting.

If you report another user, the app prepares an email in your own mail app addressed to haoyangiosdev@gmail.com. It includes the other node's public key fingerprint and the reason you choose; it includes message content only if you choose to attach it. We use reports only to respond to abuse.

7. Your control and deletion.

In the app you can delete individual memories, remove friends, and uninstall roles (which removes that role's records). To remove everything, delete the node's data directory on your server or delete the server itself. Deleting the app removes the keys stored on your phone.

8. Children.

Ownplace is not directed to children under 13.

9. Changes and contact.

We will update this page when this policy changes. Contact: haoyangiosdev@gmail.com.

所在之处 隐私政策

最后更新：2026-11-26

所在之处（下称「本 App」）由 yang hao（下称「我们」）开发。本政策说明你的信息去了哪里。

一、我们不运营服务器，也不收集你的数据。 本 App 只连接你自己拥有或租用的服务器（下称「你的节点」）。我们不运营任何接收你对话、文件、记忆或使用情况的服务器。App 内没有统计、广告或追踪代码，也不注册账号。

二、数据存在哪里。 对话、角色存下的档案、你分享的照片、记忆，都存在你的节点上。管理员权限在你手里，不在我们手里。手机上，App 把节点地址、证书指纹和访问钥匙存在系统钥匙串里，外观等偏好存在本机。

三、部署节点。 连接服务器时，App 用你输入的密码经 SSH 登录一次，安装节点软件，然后换成手机上生成的钥匙；密码只用于这一次登录，不存、不发往任何地方。服务器会从我们的下载站取节点软件；下载站只提供公开的程序文件、不接收个人数据，但和所有网站一样，其服务商可能记录发起下载的服务器 IP。

四、AI 模型与同意。 每个角色用哪个模型由你选。用你自己的推理服务器，数据不出你掌握的设施；用第三方服务商，App 会在发送任何内容之前，针对「这个角色 × 这一家」征求你的同意，不同意就一个字都不发。同意之后，相关上下文用你自己的 API key 从你的节点直接发给该服务商，受该服务商条款与隐私政策约束。你可以随时在 App 里撤回同意。

五、好友。 加好友后，消息与文件在你和对方的节点之间直接传输，端到端加密，我们不保存。

六、举报。 举报他人时，App 会在你自己的邮件 App 里写好一封发往 haoyangiosdev@gmail.com 的邮件，包含对方节点的公钥指纹与你选的原因；只有你选择附上时才包含消息内容。举报只用于处理滥用。

七、你的控制与删除。 在 App 里可以删除单条记忆、删除好友、卸载角色（同时删除该角色的档案）。要删除全部数据，删除服务器上节点的数据目录或直接删除这台服务器。删除 App 会删除手机上存的钥匙。

八、儿童。 本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童。

九、变更与联系。 政策变更时更新本页。联系：haoyangiosdev@gmail.com。
